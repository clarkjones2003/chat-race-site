Common questions

How do I race my friends? Tap Create on the home screen and share the four-character code. Your friends tap Join and type it in. Friends mode needs an internet connection; racing the squad does not.

A typo stopped me. Why? That's the rule: a wrong letter turns red and blocks you until you delete it.

How do I delete my data? Tap your avatar on the home screen to open Settings, then tap Delete my data.
