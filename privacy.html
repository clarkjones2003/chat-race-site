Chat Race — Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 September 2026

Chat Race is a typing race game. This page explains exactly what it stores, who can see it, and how to get rid of it. It is written in plain English on purpose — there is not much to say, and saying it clearly is more useful than saying it like a lawyer.

The short version. There is no sign-up. Chat Race never asks for your email, your phone number or a password, and it never asks for your contacts, your location or your photos. It stores a display name you choose, a colour you pick, and the results of races you play with friends. There are no analytics, no trackers and no advertising in the app. You can delete everything from inside the app at any time.

There is no account to sign up for

The first time you open Chat Race it creates an anonymous account for you in the background. It is a random identifier and nothing else — it is not linked to your name, your Apple ID, your email or your phone number, and there is nothing to log in to. Its only job is to let the app remember which player is you when you race friends.

What is stored, and where

On servers run by Supabase (hosted in the United States, in their US East region):

  • Your display name — whatever you typed, up to 20 characters. You choose it; the app suggests a random one and you can keep that.
  • Your colour — which of six colours you picked.
  • Rooms you create or join — the four-character room code, who is in it, and when. Room codes are reused once a room closes.
  • Results of finished matches — your points and finishing place, with your name and colour as they were at the time, so an old result still reads correctly if you rename yourself later.

On your phone only, and never sent anywhere:

  • A copy of your name and colour, so the app works before it reaches the network.
  • Your haptics and reduce-motion preferences.
  • Your best typing speed, and how many races and matches you have played and won.
  • The token for your anonymous account.

What is shared while you race

When you race friends in a room, the app sends your typing progress — how many characters of the phrase you have correct — and whether you have finished, to the other players in that room, several times a second. That is what moves your avatar along their screens.

This is not recorded. It is sent live to the people in the room and is not written down anywhere, by us or by anyone else. What you actually typed is never sent at all — only how far through the phrase you are. When the match ends, only the final points and places are saved.

The other players in a room can see your display name, your colour and your progress. That is the game. They cannot see anything else about you, because there is nothing else.

What is not collected

  • No email address, phone number or password.
  • No contacts, location, photos, microphone or camera.
  • No advertising identifier, and no advertising.
  • No analytics, no usage tracking, no crash reporting, and no third-party tracking software of any kind. The app has 17 software dependencies and not one of them is an analytics, advertising, attribution or crash-reporting library.
  • Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared with anyone for advertising.

Deleting everything

Open Settings from your avatar on the home screen, then tap Delete my data.

That immediately deletes your name, your colour, your seat in any room, and every match result you have. Any room you are currently hosting is closed. You are signed out of your anonymous account and the app starts over as if it were newly installed.

It cannot be undone, and it is not reversible by asking us — once the rows are gone they are gone.

Two honest details. First, deleting removes your result rows from matches you played, which means you also disappear from those friends' saved results for that match — there is one row per player per match and yours is yours. Second, the anonymous identifier itself remains in the authentication system as an empty record with no name, colour, results or rooms attached to it, because the app deliberately does not carry the credentials that would let it delete that record. Nothing about you is attached to it.

Deleting the app from your phone removes everything stored on the phone, but does not by itself delete what is on the server — use Delete my data first if that is what you want.

How long things are kept

Your name, colour and match results are kept until you delete them. Rooms are closed automatically once they have clearly been abandoned, which frees their code for reuse.

Children

Chat Race is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect information from them. It asks for no personal information from anyone. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us using the address below and it will be deleted.

Your rights

Depending on where you live — for example in the UK, the EU or California — you may have rights to access, correct or delete your information, and to object to how it is used. In practice, Chat Race gives everyone all of these from inside the app: you can see your name and colour on the settings screen, change them whenever you like, and delete everything with one tap. If you would rather ask, use the address below.
